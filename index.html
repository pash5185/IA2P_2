Submission Guidelines

  • All submissions must be original work not previously published in another IA²P programme.
  • Projects must have a clearly stated AI/ML component with documented methodology.
  • Code repositories should include a README and reproducibility instructions.
  • International submissions welcome in all languages — provide an English abstract.
  • Showcase listings go live within 14 business days of review approval.

What Happens Next

  • Day 1–3: Acknowledgement email sent with submission ID.
  • Day 4–14: Technical committee review for completeness and originality.
  • Day 15: Decision notification with reviewer feedback.
  • Day 16+: Approved projects go live in the public showcase.

ABOUT
Who We Are

The Association

Founded to unite the world's foremost minds in artificial intelligence — across ethics, governance, policy, and technical research.

Our Mission

Advancing Responsible AI
for All of Humanity

The International Association of AI Professionals (IA²P) was established as a global, nomination-only society for distinguished leaders advancing artificial intelligence responsibly across every sector of the global economy.

We convene technologists, ethicists, policymakers, legal scholars, and industry executives to shape the governance and deployment of AI in ways that benefit all of humanity — not just those with access to the most advanced tools.

Our work spans peer-reviewed research, international policy advocacy, cross-sector competitions, and educational programmes that build capacity in every region of the world.
